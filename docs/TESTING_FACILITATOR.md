## Opening script (read verbatim)

> This is the "support matrix" review interface of an invoice extraction system. I will not explain what
> it is. Using `runs/demo/support_panel.html` (open directly in a browser) and the files in the
> `runs/demo/` directory, complete 5 tasks. You may ask me "what does this word mean"; you may not ask me
> "what is the answer". For each task, tell me: the answer, and which file you found it in.

## Task cards (given to the subject) and answer key (you keep)

### T1 — "Why is the amount_due of invoice 00134dd3 at the very front of the review queue?"
- **Anchor**: doc `00134dd365a24343b35b78c6`, field `amount_due`, value `21,900.66`
- **Expected answer**: DWS produced a value, but the freeze transaction rejected it — the value's token
  coverage in this invoice's independent OCR is only 0.33 (<0.8), so it cannot bind; the rejection event
  `draft_binding_rejected` is in `event_log.jsonl`.
- **Pass**: says "the value was rejected at freezing + why + where the event is".
- **Common wrong answer**: "because it extracted it wrong" — the system makes no such judgment; follow up
  with "how would it know?"

### T2 — "The panel says 623 drafts were rejected. How do you verify that number?"
- **Task wording (2026-08-02 acceptance revision)**: say "**recompute** from stored evidence with a
  one-line command", not "count them yourself" — one subject read it as counting line by line by hand,
  verbatim: "that's impossible".
- **Answer**: `grep -c '"blocking": true' runs/demo/gate_report.json` (verified working),
  or count findings with `jq`/`python`. The ledger's sha256 = the content hash of the claims'
  canonicalized serialization (content addressing).
- **Pass**: the number matches + says the hash is a function of content (not a timestamp, not random).

### T3 — "Where does the system explicitly say it doesn't know? Give two classes."
- **Three acceptable classes** (each with an example):
  1. `dws_returned_no_value` (428 rows): DWS returned no value, e.g. 00134dd3's due_date;
     a blocking finding, not a "load cost"; e.g. GF-0005/GF-0006 carry the repair route vision_reread
  2. `visual_not_measured` (1,443 rows): whole-page image reading **not yet measured**, not "no problem"
  3. `draft_rejected_at_freeze` (162 rows): the value cannot bind to the page and is rejected on the spot
- **Pass**: understands that "doesn't know" is an explicitly labeled state, not missing data.

### T4 — "Can the amounts on invoice 0079863f go straight into error-rate statistics?"
- **Anchor**: doc `0079863fa739477290959e5d`
  (net $7,050.75 / gross $8,295.00 / due $7,050.75 — 0.85, the 15% agency commission signature)
- **Expected answer**: no. Paper Gross is the list price and Net the actual pay after the 15% commission;
  EN 16931's gross is the actual pay — the two vocabularies point in opposite directions; this is a
  caliber dispute, not an extraction error; the system tags `label_convention_disputed`, routes it to
  human adjudication, and keeps it out of every error rate (charter five).
- **Instant-veto grade**: if the subject says "gross disagrees with due, so gross was extracted wrong",
  the dispute dimension's presentation has failed; record it and change the panel.

### T5 — "Does this demo prove DWS extraction is trustworthy?"
- **The only correct answer**: no. It delivers mechanically verifiable support relations and does not
  promise accurate extraction; the panel-top section "what this is, what is not claimed" states
  explicitly "does not claim DWS is trustworthy".
- **Instant veto**: answering "yes" or "probably" = the whole acceptance fails; the panel's phrasing must
  change, and after the change a new subject re-tests.
